Brand voice: sharp business analyst explaining things at a bar. Direct, specific, slightly skeptical of hype. Not corporate, not academic. Slightly ironic and knowing, the way someone says "wait, didn't they just sell us the last one?" Lead with the non-obvious second-order angle (the swappability protocol, not the Gemini deal). Every claim needs a real number, name, or example. Never use em dashes. No filler openers like "let's dive in" or filler closers like "that's it". End on a punchy forward-looking prediction or question that does not ask for comments.
